# Practical Lab Assignment - Variables/Operators

1. Print "Hello, World!".
2. Print an integer entered by the user.
3. Compute Add/Subtract/Multiply/Divide Two Integers.
4. Compute Add/Subtract/Multiply/Divide Two Integers entered by the user.
5. Compute Add/Subtract/Multiply/Divide two Floating Point Numbers.
6. Compute Quotient and Remainder.
7. Compute the Area and Circumference of a Circle.
8. Compute the Area of a Scalene Triangle.
9. Compute the Area of an Equilateral Triangle.
10. Compute the Area of a Right Angle Triangle.
11. Compute the Area and Perimeter of a Rectangle.
12. Compute the Area and Perimeter of a Square.
13. Find ASCII Value of a Character.
14. Find the Size of int, float, double and char.
15. Swap Two Numbers.
